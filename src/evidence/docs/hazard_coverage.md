# Hazard coverage

| Hazard | What you track | Indicator |
|---|---|---|
| Space radiation | Per-astronaut dose from real dose rates | Exposure ledger, dose beside symptoms |
| Isolation and confinement | Mood and sleep drift | Behavioral-health domain, crew-wide drift |
| Altered gravity | Exercise-countermeasure adherence, BP/HR trend | Cardiovascular and musculoskeletal proxy (bone density cannot be measured by a daily check) |
| Closed environment | CO2 | Environment domain |
| Immune and other changes | Temperature, symptoms, blood panel replay | Immune domain |
