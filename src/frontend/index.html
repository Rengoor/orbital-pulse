Orbital Pulse.

Astronaut health monitoring UI. Every page below runs on the same tokens and component library.

Theme: NightDay (add ?theme=day to any page)

The three views

  • Helmet HUDWhat one astronaut sees through the visor: their own tier, the one authorized action and up to four vitals, all from the backend verdict. Shows NO LINK when the backend is unreachable. In hud/.
  • Tablet · crew health monitorLive from the backend: every monitored channel for a crew member, the current verdict, and why it can be trusted. No sample data. In tablet/.
  • Earth baseGround oversight with every log: decisions, chain integrity and checkpoints, downlink and reviews. Lives in earth/.

Component library

  • VitalReadoutOne channel's live value: label, tier badge, big mono number, unit, change from baseline, trend with its normal band, and source.
  • SourceLabelSays where a value came from: `REAL · NASA OSDR`, RadLab or DONKI, a hatched `SIMULATED`, or `MANUAL ENTRY`.
  • TierBadgeThe tier as icon plus word on its wash: Nominal, Watch, Act now, No data.
  • ButtonActions. `primary` (suit orange) appears at most once per view, for the action the tier demands. `secondary` (holo outline) is the default. `ghost` is for low-weight actions such as Details.
  • PanelThe instrument housing for everything on a dashboard: hull surface, hairline border, two holo corner brackets.
