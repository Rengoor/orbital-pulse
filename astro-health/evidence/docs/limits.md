# Limits (state openly)

- Daily vitals are simulated. Only baselines, radiation and the demo event use real NASA data.
- Inspiration4 is four people on a three-day flight: the replay is retrospective and does not show in-flight detection.
- The simulator is our own, so comparisons are indicative, not proof.
- Every threshold is a placeholder and needs sourcing from NASA documents and medical review.
- Decision support, not diagnosis. Not through NASA software classification, crew testing or validation on flight data.
- Earth reviewer verdicts are simulated. The demo uses a shared signing key; a real system needs asymmetric signatures.
