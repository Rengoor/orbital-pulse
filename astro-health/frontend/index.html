Vitalis.

Astronaut health monitoring UI. Every page below runs on the same tokens and component library.

Theme: NightDay (add ?theme=day to any page)

Pages

  • HelmetHUDA showcase page, not a component: a prototype of the helmet display that shows the wearer only their own vitals and flags anything that needs action.
  • MissionConsoleA showcase page, not a component: the full crew-watch console built from the kit. Switch crew, flip between Holo body, Dashboard and AR, and click the body. The decision card, advisor and readouts follow the selection. Copy its layout and data flow when building the real frontend.
  • StationTabletA showcase page, not a component: the habitat tablet dashboard, with Commander, CMO and My health roles.

Hologram

  • BodyMapThe holographic crew body: a drifting wireframe astronaut in an EVA suit (or the bare body) with tier-coloured hotspots that open a callout with the zone's reading.

Vitals

  • DoseGaugeThe mission radiation dose against its limit on a 270° dial, with the live dose rate and any space-weather event.
  • SparklineA small trend line with the normal band shaded and the latest point coloured by tier.
  • VitalReadoutOne channel's live value: label, tier badge, big mono number, unit, change from baseline, trend with its normal band, and source.

Guidance

  • AdvisoryThe LLM advisor's suggestion, streamed in as it is generated and then stamped with the Checker's result.
  • DecisionCardThe Checker's verdict for a crew member: tier, headline, the one action to take, how the advisor's proposal was confirmed or raised, reasons and provenance.

Status

  • SourceLabelSays where a value came from: `REAL · NASA OSDR`, RadLab or DONKI, a hatched `SIMULATED`, or `MANUAL ENTRY`.
  • TierBadgeThe tier as icon plus word on its wash: Nominal, Watch, Act now, No data.

Mission

  • DelayDialThe communication delay to Earth: signal packets travelling the link, one-way and round-trip time, and how soon ground could answer.

Navigation

  • CrewSwitcherChooses which crew member every panel shows. Each avatar ring and corner icon carries that person's worst tier.
  • SegmentedControlSwitches between views of the same data: Holo body, Dashboard, AR.

Actions

  • ButtonActions. `primary` (suit orange) appears at most once per view, for the action the tier demands. `secondary` (holo outline) is the default. `ghost` is for low-weight actions such as Details.

Layout

  • PanelThe instrument housing for everything on a dashboard: hull surface, hairline border, two holo corner brackets.
